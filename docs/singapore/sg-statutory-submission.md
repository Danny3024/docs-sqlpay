## 1 Central Provident Fund (CPF)

### 1.1 Setting up Company CPF Submission Number 

1. Navigate to **File** > **Company Profile**.

    ![1](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/1.png)

2. Click on **CPFB** tab.

2. Fill in **CPF Submission No. 1** and optionally **CPF Submission No. 2** if have additional CPF Submission Number.

    ![2](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/2.png)

### 1.2 Assign CPF Submission Number to Employee

:::info
Optional for company that only have one CPF Submission Number. Default CPF Submission No.1 is assigned to employees.
::: 

1. Navigate to **Human Resource** > **Maintain Employee**.

    ![3](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/3.png)

2. Click on **Payroll Info** tab.

3. Select the CPF Submission Number associated with the employee. 

    ![4](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/4.png)

### 1.3 Export CPF EZPay File

1. Navigate to **Payroll** > **Statutory Report (Monthly)** > **Print CPF + SHG + SDL Contribution**.

    ![5](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/5.png)

2. Fill in the **Year** and **Month** of submission and click ▶️**Apply**.

    ![6](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/6.png)

3. Click **Save to File** at the bottom right corner.

4. Key in the **Advice Code** and select a destination path to export to. Click **Save**. 

    ![7](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/7.png)

### 1.4 Direct Submission to CPF Board 

1. Navigate to ☁️ > **Submit CPF**.

    ![8](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/8.png)

2. Fill in the **Month** and **Year** of submission and click ▶️**Apply**.

    ![9](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/9.png)

3. The **Pending Submission** tab displays employees whose CPF contributions have not yet been submitted for the month.

4. Click **Next**.

5. Fill in the **Advice Code** and select **Payment Mode**. Fill in **Deduction Date** if select **Direct Debit** as Payment Mode.

    :::info How to Determine Direct Debit Deduction Date
    If the submission is made **before 5:15 PM**, the Deduction Date must be **2 working days** after the submission date. 
    Otherwise, it must be **3 working days** after the submission date. 
    :::

    ![10](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/10.png)

6. Click **Submit**.

7. Logon using either **Singpass App** or **Password Login**.

    ![11](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/11.png)

8. Follow wizard to submit to CPF Board.

## 2 Occupational Employment Dataset (OED)

### 2.1 Export OED File

1. Navigate to **Payroll** > **Ad Hoc Reports** > **Print OED**.

    ![12](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/12.png)

2. Fill in the **Year** and **Month** of submission. Fill in other details for filtering. Click ▶️**Apply**.

    ![13](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/13.png)

3. Click **Save to File** at the bottom right corner.

4. Select a destination path to export to. Click **Save**. 

    ![14](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/14.png)

### 2.2 Direct Submission to Ministry of Manpower 

1. Navigate to **Tools** > **Maintain User**

    ![15](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/15.png)

2. Fill in **Name** and **Email**. It will be used for requestor name and email of the submission.

    :::info
    A separate email will be sent to requestor email to ensure requestor is kept informed
    throughout the submission process
    :::

    ![16](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/16.png)    


2. Navigate to ☁️ > **Submit OED**.

    ![17](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/17.png)

3. Fill in the **Month** and **Year** of submission and click ▶️**Apply**.

    ![18](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/18.png)

4. Click **Submit**.

5. Logon using either **Singpass App** or **Password Login**.

    ![11](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/11.png)

6. Follow wizard to submit to Ministry of Manpower.

## 3 IR8A

### 3.1 Print IR8A Form

1. Navigate to **Payroll** > **Statutary Reports (Yearly)** > **Print IR8A**.

    ![19](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/19.png)

2. Fill in the **Year** of submission. Fill in other details for filtering. Click ▶️**Apply**.

    ![20](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/20.png)
   
    :::info CUSTOMIZATION
    In SQL Payroll, we support amending employee IR8A, Appendix 8A, and Appendix 8B form details. Refer to [Customize IR8A](#32-customize-ir8a) for guidance.
    :::

3. Click 🖨️ (Print).

    ![21](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/21.png)

4. Select the report to Print (IR8A, Appendix 8A or Appendix 8B).

    ![22](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/22.png)

### 3.2 Customize IR8A

1. Navigate to **Payroll** > **Statutary Reports (Yearly)** > **Print IR8A**.

    ![19](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/19.png)

2. Fill in the **Year** of submission. Fill in other details for filtering. Click ▶️**Apply**.

    ![20](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/20.png)

3. Click on **Open IR8A** at bottom right corner. Click on **Open Appendix 8A** or **Open Appendix 8B** to amend other forms.

    ![23](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/23.png)

4. Select the employee to amend and click **New**.

    ![24](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/24.png)

5. Fill in the details to amend and click 💾 (Save).

    ![25](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/25.png)

6. The amended value will replace the original value.

    ![26](../../static/img/singapore/sg-statutory-submission/26.png)
